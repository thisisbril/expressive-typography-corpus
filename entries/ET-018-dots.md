    primitve_relationship_explained: Mutation amplifies Inflection. The fill-state inversion of the o (Inflection)makes it read as a solid dot, which is the thing the word names. The o's scale and forward position cause its boundary to overlap and interrupt the stems of the d and the t (Mutation), embedding the dot physically into the word's structure and making its eventually noticed.
target_glyphs:
  - o (primary)
  - d and t (sites of consequent Mutation)
operation_description: >
  The counter of the o is rendered black, while its bowl is white. This color inversion produces a pure dot. The letterform is scaled and positioned so that its circular boundary overlaps the stem of the d on its left and the stem of the t on its right, interrupting the geometry of both letterforms at the points of contact. The o's shape is geometrically intact but its color and its relationship to neighboring letterforms have both been transformed.
legibility_integrity: Contextual
surprise_legibility_ratio: 3
curatorial_note: >
  A self-referential operation whose legibility cost is real and analytically interesting. The o presents as a pure dot. Its letterform identity is fully suspended by the color inversion and is only recoverable as a letter through the stem interruptions it produces in the d and t. The Mutation on d and t is the only evidence that th o letterform is present at all. Without it, the word reads as d•ts with a decorative spot. The surprise arrives immediately, but the legibility requires reconstruction. That gap is what holds this at a 3, and what makes the Mutation's role structurally essential.
generative_principle: >
  When a word names a visual object that corresponds to the natural geometry of one of its letterforms, examine whether a fill-state or color change alone is sufficient to complete the iconic reading. When it is, the operation achieves maximum economy. No geometry is altered and no external element imposed. Only the condition of the letterform changes. The reading is strongest when the transformed letterform is then embedded into the word's structure through overlap or contact rather than left isolated.
