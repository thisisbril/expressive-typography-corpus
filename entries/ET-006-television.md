operation_description: >
  The V letterform retains its standard geometry. There are two diagonal arms meeting at a central vertex at the baseline. From the V's upper terminals, two thinner lines extend outward and upward beyond the cap height, diverging asymmetrically and each terminating in a small circular dot. The resulting form reads as a complete television antenna. The V body functions as the antenna base and lower support structure, the extended lines function as the rabbit ear arms, and the circular dots function as the antenna tips. The V is both a legible letterform and a complete iconic representation of the object the word names.
concept_encoded: A television antenna. The physical object most iconically associated with the technology the word names.
semantic_mechanism: >
  The V is the only letterform in Television whose geometry already contains the structural core of a television antenna. The Extension operation completes the iconic reading by adding the rabbit ear lines and tip dots that the V's existing geometry implies but does not fully contain. The result is a Substitution-by-recognition. The V was always an antenna.
legibility_integrity: Full
surprise_legibility_ratio: 5
curatorial_note: >
  The asymmetry of the antenna arms deserves specific attention. A symmetric V extended symmetrically would produce a more formally resolved antenna but a less convincing one. Real rabbit ear antennas are adjusted asymmetrically to tune reception. This usually results in one arm extended further than the other. The asymmetry makes the antenna feel used, functional, and realistic. This is a detail that operates below conscious recognition for most viewers but contributes materially to why the piece feels inevitable. The difference between a good expressive typographic piece and an exceptional one often lives at exactly this level of detail.
generative_principle: >
  When a letterform's native geometry already contains the structural core of a real-world object associated with the word's meaning, the designer's task is recognition. Identify what the letterform already is, then supply only the elements needed to make that identity fully legible as an iconic object.
