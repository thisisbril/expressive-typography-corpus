  Distance is a interval between two positions. The T letterform contains a horizontal crossbar whose natural axis aligns precisely with the concept of a span. The designer extends that crossbar until it reaches the far edges of the word's interior, making DIS and ANCE the two endpoints and the T the measured interval between them. The junctions with S and A anchor the crossbar to the word's body, which ensures the Extension reads as a structural connection.

  The T performs distance as a measurable geometric fact within the typography itself. The word is literally as wide as the concept it names. The Mutation in S and A sharpens this reading further. The designer recognized that the T's crossbar was always capable of this operation.
generative_principle: >
  When a word names a spatial or relational concept like distance, span, gap, examine whether any letterform contains a stroke whose natural axis aligns with the concept's spatial character. Extension works most powerfully when it requires no additional geometry besides the release of an existing stroke beyond its conventional boundary. When that extended stroke reaches adjacent letterforms, the resulting junctions can structurally anchor the operation and make the extended stroke not visually suspended.
