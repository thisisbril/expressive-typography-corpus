  Each letterform from the first I onward is rendered at a progressively reduced opacity, beginning at full black and fading through successive grey values until the letterforms approach white against the white background. The final letterform, E, is absent entirely. Its position in the sequence is empty. The geometry of every letterform is intact. Only their opacity states and ultimate presence change across the word's length.
concept_encoded: Invisibility. The progressive loss of visual presence until nothing remains.
semantic_mechanism: >
  Something does not become invisible all at once. It fades. The design enacts this gradient literally. Each letterform's opacity is the word's own measure of how invisible it has become at that position. By the time the sequence reaches the E, the word has enacted its own meaning so completely that the final letter has ceased to exist. The word performs its own definition by disappearing before it can finish spelling itself.

This way the final letterform is not faded but absent, thereby converting a visual effect into a conceptual statement about the condition the word names.
