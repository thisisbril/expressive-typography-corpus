operation_description: >
  The nn pair has been replaced by a three-dimensional tunnel structure rendered in ochre. The arched tunnel mouth whose arch geometry is derived directly from the n's own arch form is scaled up and given depth and perspective. The tunnel structure sits where the nn pair would stand, inferrable as two n's through word context. The entire word is simultaneously rotated to a diagonal orientation, departing from a conventional horizontal baseline, so that the letterforms appear in an isometric perspective. Every letterform's orientation has been changed without altering any individual geometry.
concept_encoded: A tunnel; the spatial experience of depth and passage through
  an arched structure
semantic_mechanism: >
  The nn pair is the geometric opportunity in *tunnel* for two compounding reasons. First, the word contains two n's in sequence. The doubled arch of the n's is already the tunnel's cross-section repeated. Second, the n's arch is structurally identical to a tunnel mouth in profile. A rounded top, two vertical legs, an opening below. Scaling the arch and rendering it in three dimensions requires minimal geometric displacement because the letterform already contains the tunnel's defining shape. The diagonal orientation of the full word then enacts the tunnel's spatial property of depth.

curatorial_note: >
  A compound operation whose two primitives work across different scales simultaneously. The Substitution operates locally on the nn pair and transforms two the letterforms into the object the word names. The Inflection operates globally on the entire word and transforms the reading experience from observation into spatial immersion. The n's arch was always a tunnel mouth and the designer recognized that the doubled arch was already a tunnel waiting to be rendered. The diagonal orientation is what elevates this beyond a clever substitution into a spatial proposition.
generative_principle: >
  When a word names a spatial structure, examine whether any letterform's arch or enclosing geometry already approximates that structure's cross-section. Doubled letterforms are strongest candidates. Repetition provides both the geometric material and the spatial suggestion of extension into depth. When Substitution produces the object, a global Inflection of the word's orientation can then enact the object's spatial experience rather than just its appearance.
