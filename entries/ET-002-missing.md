Two gaps where two bodies should stand, marked only by the dots that float above them like evidence of what was there. The choice to suppress both i's rather than one is precise. A single missing stem reads as a typographic anomaly, but two floating dots establish a pattern that makes the operation undeniable and the concept unavoidable.
