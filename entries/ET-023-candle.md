operation_description: >
  The A's upper counter, which is normally triangular is reshaped into a flame form. The lower counter of the left and right stems has also been reshaped. This modification makes the lower counter of the A reads as the body of a candle. All geometric modifications occur within the A's typographic boundary. No external iconic element has been imposed. The candle and flame are produced entirely by reshaping the A's existing geometry.
concept_encoded: A candle with a flame. Light, heat, the act of burning
semantic_mechanism: >
  The A is the geometric opportunity in *CANDLE* for two compounding reasons. First, its triangular upper counter, which creates a space whose form already approximates the profile of a teardrop-shaped flame. It is also the ideal spot for the flame to be positioned. Second is the space of the lower counter. Its normally narrow, converging walls is cylindrical and approximates a candle body. In a nutshell, the A's structure was always a latent candle. The Mutation of both counters of the letterform simply completes what the geometry already suggested.

curatorial_note: >
  An economical Mutation in which the iconic reading is produced entirely from within the letterform's existing geometry. The A's lower counter becomes the candle body, and the reshaped upper counter becomes the flame. What makes this exceptional is that the A's natural geometry is compatible with the candle reading. It actively suggests it.
generative_principle: >
  When a word names a physical object, examine whether any letterform's counter or negative space already approximates that object's silhouette under minimal deformation. The counter is an underexploited site of semantic operation. Designers habitually work with strokes and terminals while leaving the negative space inside the letterform untouched. When the counter's geometry is already proximate to the concept, reshaping it requires less intervention than working from the outside in, and the resulting reading feels more inevitable because it emerges from within the letterform.
