operation_description: >
  The I has been displaced upward from the baseline, its lower terminal sitting at approximately cap height of the surrounding letterforms while its upper terminal rises above them. The letterform's geometry is entirely intact. A hairline wire descends from beyond the upper frame of the composition, connecting to the I's upper terminal and suspending it above the baseline. The remaining letterforms L, F, T sit at their conventional baseline positions, unchanged.
concept_encoded: An object suspended from above, the act of lifting by a line.
semantic_mechanism: >
  Lift names an act of vertical displacement, something moved upward from its resting position. The I is the only letterform in *LIFT* whose natural geometry is both structurally identical to a suspended object and positionally displaceable without destroying the word's legibility. Elevating it above the baseline enacts the displacement. The hairline wire connecting its upper terminal to the space above specifies the mechanism. It has been held from above and raised. The gap left between the I and the baseline is the distance it has traveled. The word names the act and its most geometrically receptive letterform undergoes it, visibly, within the word that names it.
legibility_integrity: Full
surprise_legibility_ratio: 5
curatorial_note: >
  A compound operation whose two primitives are additively related. The wire explains the elevation. What makes this exceptional is the wire's restraint. It is a hairline, almost invisible, the minimum geometry required to establish causality. The lighter the wire, the more the I appears to float and the more the act of lifting feels effortless, which is precisely what the word means at its most aspirational register.
generative_principle: >
  When a word names an act of vertical displacement, examine whether any letterform can be elevated above the baseline while remaining legible through word context. Inflection performs the displacement. A minimal non-typographic connector like a wire, a thread, a beam, then enables the Substitution that converts the displacement from an unexplained gap into a caused event. The connector is strongest when it is as minimal as possible. The less geometry it uses to establish causality, the more inevitable the reading feels.
