operation_description: >
  The t's crossbar has been structurally modified at its left terminal. The stroke curves downward rather than terminating cleanly, leaving a narrow horizontal aperture or slot in the crossbar. The modification is internal to the t's typographic boundary. Every other letterform is intact and standard. The opening in the crossbar produces a slot form of a horizontal stroke with a gap through which things could pass.
concept_encoded: A bank teller's transaction slot; the narrow horizontal aperture through which cash and documents are exchanged.
semantic_mechanism: >
  A bank teller's defining architectural feature is the horizontal slot in the counter or window through which money, cards, and documents pass between teller and customer. The t's crossbar is already a horizontal stroke. It is geometrically proximate to a slot in its natural form. Opening its terminal to produce a narrow aperture transforms the crossbar into that slot with minimal geometric displacement. The Mutation encodes the teller's functional interface (the physical mechanism of the transaction) within the word's first and most structurally operative letterform.
