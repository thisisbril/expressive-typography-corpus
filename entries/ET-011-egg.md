    primitive_relationship_explained: Mutation enables Substitution. The bowl of the e is reshaped into a closed oval approximating an egg's silhouette (Mutation), creating the geometric condition that allows the crack motif replacing the crossbar (Substitution) to complete an unambiguous iconic reading of a cracked egg. Without the Mutation establishing the egg-shell profile, the substituted crack would read as an arbitrary stroke replacement.
target_glyphs:
  - e
operation_description: >
  The e's bowl is reshaped into a closed oval approximating an egg's profile, and its conventional crossbar is replaced by a lightning-bolt crack motif running diagonally through the interior of the form. The outer curve of the e becomes the egg's shell. The crack occupies the space where the typographic crossbar would sit. Together the two operations transform the e into a cracked egg while the letterform retains sufficient typographic identity to read clearly as an e within the word.  
concept_encoded: A cracked egg; hatching; breakage from within
semantic_mechanism: >
  The e is the first and most geometrically receptive letterform in *egg* for this operation. The bowl of an e already approximates the profile of an egg when the opening is closed and the form is rounded.The crossbar, which is the e's most structurally distinctive internal element, occupies precisely the position where a crack would run across an egg's surface. Replacing it with a crack motif requires minimal geometric displacement, which produces a complete iconic reading. The word names the object, and the first letter becomes it.
legibility_integrity: Full
surprise_legibility_ratio: 3
curatorial_note: >
  A clean and economical compound operation. The Mutation does an essential preparatory work. Without the bowl being closed into an egg profile, the crack reads as a deformed crossbar rather than a crack in a shell. The Substitution then completes the iconic reading with precision. What keeps this at a 3 rather than a 5 is that the conceptual logic is immediately transparent. The first letter of egg becomes an egg. The surprise is present but the path to it is short. A 5 would require the surprise and legibility to hold their tension longer before resolving.
generative_principle: >
  When a word names a physical object, examine whether any letterform's existing bowl or counter geometry approximates that object's silhouette under minimal deformation. If so, the interior of that bowl becomes available for Substitution. The typographic stroke that normally occupies it can be replaced by a detail that completes the iconic reading. The e is a strong candidate for this operation because its near-closed bowl is geometrically proximate to many rounded natural forms.
